Currently I am working as a Ph.D. researcher at UC Irvine with Prof. <a href='https://sitaohuang.com/'>Sitao Huang</a> and Prof. <a href='https://hyoukjunkwon.com/'>Hyoukjun Kwon</a>. My research interests include acceleration of emerging agentic ML workloads and efficiency of domain-specific computer architectures. I am passionate about designing deep learning solutions to challenging problems and deploying them to low-power devices. During my PhD, I have interned at <a href='https://www.microsoft.com/applied-sciences'>Microsoft Applied Science Group</a> and <a href='https://tech.facebook.com/reality-labs/'>Meta Reality Labs</a>.

Previously, I worked as an Embedded AI Research Engineer at <a href='https://www.linkedin.com/company/deeplite/'>Deeplite (now STMicroelectronics)</a> in Toronto. My primary responsibilities included working with compilers and runtimes to deploy Deep Learning vision models in low-power embedded devices. I have completed my Master of Science in Communication Engineering with a focus on hardware acceleration for inference at the <a href='https://www.tum.de/en/'>Technical University of Munich</a> under Prof. <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=de&user=h7erxnkAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate'>Walter Stechele</a>.
